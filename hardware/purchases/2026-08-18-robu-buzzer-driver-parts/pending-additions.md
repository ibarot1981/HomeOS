## Reported as added to cart

### 2.54 mm breakaway male header

The AMS1117 reference image shows two bare holes at each end and no fitted pins.
Use four pins from a straight, single-row, breakable 2.54 mm male header so the
module can be connected securely on a breadboard.

The user reported adding Berg-strip male connectors on 2026-08-18. Exact pitch,
orientation, quantity, price, and product image could not be recaptured because
the Chrome tab was no longer available to the browser connection.

- Robu option: [1x40 Berg Strip Male Connector - 10pcs](https://robu.in/product/1x40-berg-strip-male-connector/)
- Required properties: 2.54 mm pitch, straight, single-row, male, through-hole,
  breakable
- Do not buy a 2.00 mm or 1.27 mm strip

## Confirmed already owned

### Male-to-female jumpers

At least three are needed from the male-pin ESP32-S3 board to breadboard rows for
5 V, GND, and GPIO17.

The user confirmed having male-to-female jumper wires on 2026-08-18.

- Robu category/result: [10-wire male-to-female jumper set, 20 cm](https://robu.in/product-category/batteries/wires-and-cables/multi-color-project-cables/dupont-cable/)
- Required properties: 2.54 mm Dupont, male at one end, female at the other

### Breadboard

The existing small breadboard may suffice, but its fit has not been recorded. A
400-point or 830-point solderless board gives comfortable room for the regulator,
transistor, diode, resistors, and capacitors.

The user confirmed having a new 840-point breadboard on 2026-08-18. No additional
breadboard is required for this circuit.

- Robu option: [transparent 400-point solderless breadboard](https://robu.in/product/transparent-400-points-solderless-breadboard/)
- Alternative category: [breadboards and zero PCBs](https://robu.in/product-category/electronic-components/breadboard-and-zero-pcb/)
- Required properties: 2.54 mm solderless contacts and enough distinct rows for
  the driver circuit
